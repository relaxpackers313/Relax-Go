## Customers
- Requests can be cancelled free of charge in the app before the trip starts.
- Relax Go does not collect trip fares; fare disputes are between you and the driver, though Support will assist and act on misconduct.

## Drivers — wallet and credits
1. **Verified recharges** are credited in full to your wallet. If money left your account but the wallet was not credited within 30 minutes (a delayed gateway confirmation), the credit lands automatically once the gateway confirms; if it does not, raise a Support ticket with the payment reference — verified cases are credited or refunded to source within 5–7 working days.
2. **Wrong call charges** (e.g. charged twice for the same request within the free repeat window) are refunded to the wallet after verification against the call ledger.
3. **Unused paid balance** is refundable to your payment source on account closure, on request, within 7–10 working days, after deducting charges already incurred. **Free and promotional credits have no cash value** and are not refundable or transferable.
4. Refunds are always processed to the original payment method or the wallet — never to a third party.

## How to claim
Raise the issue from Support in the app (category: wallet/billing) or email bookrelaxpackers@gmail.com with your registered number and the transaction reference. Every wallet movement has a ledger entry we can trace.
