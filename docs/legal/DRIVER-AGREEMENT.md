# Relax Go — Driver Agreement (including Wallet, Credit & Call Usage Terms)

> **DRAFT — for review by qualified legal counsel before production use.**

Between the registered driver ("you") and **Relax Group** (operator of Relax Go, a ChefoTech product), Cuttack, Odisha, India.

## 1. Your relationship with the Platform
You are an independent service provider, not an employee, agent or partner of Relax Group or ChefoTech. Relax Go supplies you customer **leads**; the transport service, its fare and its performance are yours.

## 2. Onboarding requirements
To be approved you must provide, truthfully and completely: full name and address; a working mobile number verified by OTP; the documents configured at registration (Aadhaar, driving licence with validity, vehicle RC, insurance, and any others shown in the app); and vehicle details. Our team may approve, reject with a reason, or request corrections. **You receive customer requests only after approval.** Keeping documents valid is your responsibility: an expired critical document can remove you from customer discovery until replaced.

## 3. Going online — location sharing
While you set yourself ONLINE, your device shares your live location with the Platform, including in the background, so customers can find you and track their pickup. Going OFFLINE stops sharing immediately. Deliberately spoofing your location is ground for suspension.

## 4. Leads and calls
- Customers choose drivers from a nearest-first list; a request sent to you is a **lead**.
- Customer phone numbers are provided **only through the in-app call flow**, and every such call is recorded as a billable event (the call itself is a normal phone call; we log the event, not the audio).
- Misusing a customer's number (calls or messages outside serving the request) is ground for immediate suspension.

## 5. Credits, wallet and charges
- **Free credits:** you receive the welcome credits shown at approval, and any recurring free-call allowance (e.g. first N calls per day) currently configured. The app always shows what a call will cost **before** you place it.
- **Paid calls:** once free credits and allowances are exhausted, each new customer contact is charged at the rate shown in the app (rates may vary by vehicle type, city, zone or time; repeat calls on the same request within the configured window are not charged again).
- **Wallet:** recharges are made through the payment gateway shown in the app and are credited only after the payment is verified by the gateway. Every balance change appears in your transaction history. The wallet is a prepaid service balance, not a deposit; it does not earn interest.
- **Refunds:** see the Cancellation & Refund Policy. Wrongly charged calls, once verified, are refunded to the wallet.

## 6. Conduct and safety
You agree to: hold a valid licence and drive a legal, insured vehicle; treat customers respectfully; not drive under the influence; follow traffic law; and cooperate with safety investigations. Ratings below the review threshold flag your account for human review — no automatic suspension is made on ratings alone.

## 7. Suspension and termination
We may suspend or block your account for document fraud, location spoofing, customer-contact misuse, safety complaints, or payment fraud — with the reason recorded and visible to you. You may stop using the Platform at any time; remaining paid wallet balance is refundable on request per the Refund Policy.

## 8. Taxes
You are responsible for your own taxes on earnings from customers. Platform charges (call credits) include applicable GST where stated on the receipt.

## 9. Disputes
Raise billing or lead disputes through in-app Support first; see the Grievance & Support Policy for escalation. Governing law: India; courts at Cuttack, Odisha.
