# Relax Go — Safety Guidelines

> **DRAFT — for review by qualified legal counsel before production use.**

## What the Platform provides
- **SOS screen** in the customer app that dials India's national emergency number **112** from your own phone (the call is placed by you; Relax Go does not operate an emergency service and claims no integration with emergency services).
- **Trip sharing:** an active trip has a share link showing trip status, driver name, vehicle number and live vehicle position — no phone numbers.
- **Verified drivers:** every driver's identity, licence, RC and insurance are reviewed by the Relax Group team before approval, and documents are re-checked on expiry.
- **Vehicle verification:** always match the registration number in the app with the vehicle before boarding.
- **Tracked contact:** customer numbers are exchanged only through logged call events, so misuse is attributable.
- **Report & review:** every safety report goes to a human; drivers flagged for low ratings or complaints are reviewed by staff — never judged by an algorithm alone.

## For customers
Share your trip on longer journeys; sit where you are comfortable; confirm the fare before starting; report anything wrong from Support (category: safety) — safety tickets are prioritised.

## For drivers
Emergencies: dial 112. Report threatening customer behaviour through Support; abusive customer sessions are blocked. Keep your emergency contact updated in your profile.
