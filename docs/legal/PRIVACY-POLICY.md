## 1. The short version
- Customers use Relax Go **without an account**. Your identity on the Platform is an anonymous session on your device.
- Location is the most sensitive data we touch. **Customer location** is used only while the app is open, to show nearby drivers and set your pickup. **Driver location** is shared continuously — including in the background — but **only while the driver has switched themselves online**; going offline stops sharing immediately.
- Phone numbers are exchanged only through **tracked calls**: a driver never sees a customer's number on a request card.

## 2. What we collect
| Who | Data | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Device/session identifier, app version, platform | Operate the session, prevent abuse |
| Customer | Location while the app is in use | Show nearby drivers, set pickup, live tracking of an accepted request |
| Customer | Mobile number (only when you share it on a request) | Let the chosen driver contact you |
| Customer | Requests, ratings, support messages | Provide the service, quality and safety |
| Driver | Identity, licence, vehicle and other onboarding documents | Verification and legal compliance |
| Driver | Live location while online (foreground and background) | Customer discovery and tracking — the core service |
| Driver | Calls, leads, wallet transactions | Billing, receipts, dispute resolution |

## 3. What we do NOT do
- No sale of personal data.
- No advertising use of location data.
- No background collection of customer location.
- No driver access to customer numbers outside tracked calls.

## 4. Retention
- Driver location history is retained for a limited operational period (30 days by default) and then deleted automatically; the live position is overwritten continuously.
- Leads, calls and wallet records are retained as required for accounting and dispute resolution.
- A customer can delete their device session from Settings; the device is then no longer linked to past requests.
- Driver documents are retained while the driver account is active and as required by law thereafter.

## 5. Who can see what (internal)
Administrative access is role-based. Viewing live locations or location history requires specific permissions granted per staff member, and sensitive actions are recorded in an audit log.

## 6. Sharing
We share data only with: the driver/customer you are connected with (minimum needed: name, vehicle, live position during an active request); service providers under contract (cloud hosting, SMS/OTP, payments, push notifications); and authorities where the law requires it.

## 7. Your rights
Contact us at bookrelaxpackers@gmail.com or through in-app Support to access, correct or delete your data, or to raise a complaint. See the Grievance & Support Policy for timelines and escalation.

## 8. Permissions the apps request
- **Customer app:** Location (while in use), notifications, phone dialer (opens your dialer; the call is made by you).
- **Driver app:** Location including background ("Allow all the time") — required for the core service while online; camera/photos for documents; notifications; phone dialer.
