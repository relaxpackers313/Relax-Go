# Relax Go — Customer Terms

> **DRAFT — for review by qualified legal counsel before production use.**

These terms supplement the [Terms of Service](TERMS-OF-SERVICE.md) for customers of Relax Go, operated by Relax Group (Cuttack, Odisha).

## 1. Using Relax Go
- No account is needed. Your device holds an anonymous session; you can delete it in Settings at any time.
- The map shows real, currently-online drivers near you, sorted nearest first. Availability changes moment to moment.
- When you request a driver you may share a mobile number so the driver can call you. It is shown to the driver only through the tracked call flow.

## 2. The service you receive
Relax Go connects you with an independent driver; the ride itself, including the fare, is agreed between you and the driver. Confirm the fare before starting. The vehicle number shown in the app lets you verify you are boarding the right vehicle.

## 3. Fair use
Do not place requests you do not intend to pursue, harass drivers, or misuse driver contact information. Sessions that abuse the Platform are blocked.

## 4. Cancellations
You can cancel a request in the app at any time before the trip starts. There is no platform cancellation fee; repeated no-shows may limit your access.

## 5. Safety
- The SOS screen dials the national emergency number (112) from **your** phone.
- Share your live trip with family using the trip-share link when a trip is active.
- Report any incident through in-app Support; safety reports are prioritised.

## 6. Ratings
After connecting with a driver you can rate them once per request. Ratings feed driver quality review — please rate honestly.
